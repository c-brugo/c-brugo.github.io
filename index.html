About me

I studied Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, specialising in Data Analytics, and wrote my master's thesis at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid.

I joined Moviri in 2022 as an Analyst, working on data engineering and data science projects: Splunk pipelines and dashboards, cloud ingestion on AWS and GCP, and machine learning models. Since 2025 I work as an AI Consultant, focusing on AI agents and on running large language models in production.

I've always been curious about machine learning, especially its research side, and I like to learn a bit of everything.

Outside work I give back to the communities that helped me as a student. I lead the Tech Talks team at LeadTheFuture, where I've hosted 30+ webinars with professionals from around the world, and I've mentored startup teams at the Silicon Valley Study Tour hackathons. More about my activities
